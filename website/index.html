WEIGHTED CALCULATION

How Course Credits Affect Your GPA

GPA is weighted by course credit value. A course with a larger credit weight contributes more to the overall result than a course with a smaller credit weight. This calculator reads the credit weight associated with the course you select, so you do not need to enter it manually.

ORGANIZE YOUR RECORD

Yearly and Cumulative GPA

Organize courses into 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, or Other. The GPA dashboard calculates each populated year separately and combines all entered courses for your cumulative GPA.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my UofT GPA?

Add a valid UofT course, select its academic year, enter a percentage grade, and choose Calculate GPA.

Can I calculate my cumulative UofT GPA?

Yes. The GPA dashboard combines all courses you enter into a credit-weighted cumulative GPA.

Can I calculate GPA for multiple years?

Yes. Courses can be organized into 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, and Other, with a GPA shown for each populated year.

Do I need to enter course credits manually?

No. The calculator uses the credit weight attached to the selected course in its course database.

Can I use percentage grades?

Yes. Enter a percentage from 0 to 100 and the calculator converts it to a letter grade and GPA value for the calculation.

Does the calculator contain UofT courses?

Yes. Course search uses the included UofT course database to validate course selections and retrieve credit weights.
